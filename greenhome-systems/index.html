FAQ

Questions we get every week

What size system do I need?

It depends on your daily kWh usage and what you want to keep running. As a rough guide: a 5kW inverter with 5kWh of storage covers essentials in a small home, 8kW with 10kWh covers most of a three-bedroom house, and anything with geysers or pool pumps on backup needs 12kW and up.

Can I start small and add later?

Yes. Hybrid inverters are built for it — start with a backup battery, then add panels or a second battery when the budget allows. We size the inverter and cabling up front so the expansion doesn't mean replacing hardware.

How long does installation take?

A standard residential install is one to two days on site. Larger three-phase or commercial systems run three to five days, depending on roof access and DB board work.

Do you supply a Certificate of Compliance?

Every installation is signed off by a registered electrician and handed over with a CoC — which is what your insurer and your municipality will ask for.

What warranties apply?

Warranties are carried by the manufacturers: typically 10 years on panels' product warranty with a 25-year performance guarantee, 5–10 years on inverters and 10 years on lithium batteries. We handle the claim process on your behalf.

Do you install outside Gauteng?

Yes — we are based in Johannesburg and install nationwide. Out-of-province jobs carry a travel component, which is quoted up front.
