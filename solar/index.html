How an install actually goes

Four steps, about eight weeks end to end. Most of it is paperwork we handle.

Site survey

We measure the roof, check the rafters and shade-map the array across a full year. Half an hour on site, no charge.

Design & permits

You get a panel layout, a production estimate and a fixed price. We file the permit and the utility interconnection.

Install day

Mounts, panels, inverter and meter in one visit for most homes. Power is off for about two hours.

Switch on & monitor

The utility signs off, the system goes live, and per-panel monitoring lands in your account the same day.

Why people do this

The bill is the usual reason. It stops being the main one after a few years.

📉

Lower bills from day one

Generation offsets what you'd otherwise buy. The saving starts the month the meter is swapped, not after payback.

A hedge against rate rises

Retail power has climbed around 3–4% a year for a decade. The part of your usage solar covers stops tracking that.

Power through outages

Pair the array with a battery and a hybrid inverter and the essential circuits ride through a grid failure.

An owned system transfers with the property, warranty included. Nothing to novate, no lease for a buyer to assume.
