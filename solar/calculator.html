How this is calculated
  • Annual usage is your bill ÷ your rate, held flat across the 25 years.
  • The array is sized to cover that usage, then capped at what the roof holds. Panels are 440 W.
  • Production is system size × 365 × peak sun hours × 0.8, and the 0.8 is scaled again by roof exposure. That 0.8 covers inverter, temperature, soiling and wiring losses.
  • Installed cost is $2.80 per watt before incentives, less a 30% federal clean energy credit.
  • Retail rates rise 3.5% a year; panel output falls 0.5% a year.
  • Generation offsets usage at the retail rate. Anything generated beyond what you use in a year earns nothing — real export tariffs vary too much to guess at.
  • No finance costs, no maintenance, no inverter replacement, and no state or utility incentives beyond the federal credit. A real quote will differ; this is a demo.
